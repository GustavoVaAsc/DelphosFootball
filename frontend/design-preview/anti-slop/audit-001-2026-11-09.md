## What already works (keep)

- The pitch diagrams (mini pitch per row, cluster pitch banners) are the identity motif. Nothing else in the UI says football this clearly. Keep and refine them; do not add more decoration around them.
- Master-detail fits the job: scan the market, open a player, see the verdict and the replacements.
- Tabular numerals, the `prefers-reduced-motion` guard, the global `:focus-visible` ring, keyboard-reachable rows (index.html:1743), and FlagCDN images instead of emoji flags.
- No em dashes anywhere. No API routes or snake_case identifiers in the UI text.
- Motion is restrained (160-180ms, no loops) and matches a calm MOTION 1.
- The savings math is consistent with the contract (150 - 40 = 110, -73%).
