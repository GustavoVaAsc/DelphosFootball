# DESIGN.md - DelphosFootball (Football front office, light mode)

## 1. Visual theme and atmosphere
- **Archetype**: Football front-office and scouting suite for a Sporting Director (`Director Deportivo`). Desktop web only (see §4).
- **References, not templates**: Transfermarkt ES market tables (dense rows, price first), FotMob player page (one player, one verdict, stats grid), The Athletic data graphics (warm paper, ink, one accent per meaning).
- **Dial**: `Dial: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1` (a calm executive tool that is read for minutes at a time, not a landing page).
- **Mood**: Printed match-day report. Ink on warm paper, figures set like a scoreboard, colour only where money moves. Every label uses natural football terminology in executive Spanish; no API routes, no SQL column identifiers, no debug text appear in the interface.
- **Why these choices**:
  - Colour: warm paper and navy ink read as editorial sports press; green and red are kept for the one question the product answers (is this player cheap or expensive), so they stay legible as signals.
  - Type: a condensed grotesque for names and euro figures echoes kit numbers and scoreboards and fits long names in narrow columns; a serif for stored scouting reports separates long-form dossier prose from the UI tables and chat bubbles.
  - Layout: the list is the default view, and the dossier opens on demand, so the table has the full width for scanning.
  - Cards: flat bordered containers, one level deep, because the content is tables and figures, not objects that float.
- **Football identity signals**:
  - Mini pitch glyph (`36x24px`) per table row, marking the player's zone (`Ataque`, `Mediocampo`, `Defensa`). A horizontal `105:68` pitch seen from above with the outline, halfway line, centre circle, both penalty areas, both goal areas and the goals drawn outside the goal lines. Pitch lines in neutral warm grey; the zone filled in ink at low opacity with an ink outline. No zone hues: the zone's position on the pitch already carries the meaning. At `36x24px` the glyph drops what would render as sub-pixel noise (centre and penalty spots, penalty arcs, corner arcs) and keeps the rest.
  - Archetype exposure profiles in view 3, displaying empirical competition-minute shares (`Liga`, `UEFA`, `Copas`) and `Tasa de titularidad` in ink tones on a `--surface-subtle` ground instead of positional pitch zones.
  - Badge hierarchy: player portrait (`44x44px` in table, `64x64px` in dossier) + club crest (`club_logo_url`) + nationality flag (`<img>` from FlagCDN) + preferred foot (`Diestro`, `Zurdo`, `Ambidiestro`). The crest is a `20px` disc over the portrait in the table; in the dossier it is a `28px` crest with no disc, inline before the club name, and the league logo (`28px`) sits inline before the league name. Valuation status has no badge: it is plain coloured text (§2).

Rules:
- The valuation status ("Infravalorado", "En precio", "Sobrevalorado") is plain text with no ground, no pill and no border: `--up` for Infravalorado, `--down` for Sobrevalorado, and ink for "En precio" (`fair`), with no hue. The word always names the verdict, so colour is never the only signal. The `Estado` segmented filter is a control, not a status mark, and keeps its look.
- The model estimate figure ("Valor estimado") takes the colour of the verdict (`valuation_status`), never of the raw gap: `--up` for Infravalorado, `--down` for Sobrevalorado, ink for En precio. Each player shows exactly one verdict: an "En precio" player whose estimate sits 27% above price stays ink.- `valuation_status` is the only verdict. The two classifiers (Random Forest: `rf_undervalued_prob`, `rf_undervalued_pred`; decision tree: `dt_undervalued_pred`) are a separate check that answers only "undervalued or not". They never get a status word, green or red; their lines are neutral ink text (§4, Valuation).
- Comparator deltas against the reference use `--up` / `--down` only for output metrics where more is unambiguously better (minutes, goals, assists, starter share, win share). Age deltas stay neutral in `--ink-3`. The price row has no delta: every candidate is cheaper by construction, and the savings row already states the difference.
- The archetype share bar uses ink tones only (`--ink`, `--ink-2`, `--control-border`), separated by 2px gaps. No new hues.
- "Activo élite" is not shown anywhere in the interface (dossier or comparator). The flag is kept in the data contract (`is_elite_asset`); the UI does not show or use it.
- Text links ("Ver en el explorador", "Ir al Explorador de Mercado") are ink text, always underlined: `--control-border` at rest, ink on hover.
- Focus ring (2px, offset 2px) and the selected row (ink tint plus a 3px inset ink bar) use ink, not green. On the navy sidebar the focus ring is white.
- Replacement savings are shown in ink: a cheaper candidate is a price difference, not a valuation verdict.
- The shimmer band of the chat's working labels is the one exception to the contrast figures in this table; §5 specifies it.

## 3. Typography and hierarchy
- **Archivo** (Google Fonts, variable, `wdth 62..125`, `wght 100..900`):
  - Condensed cut (`font-stretch: 80%`) for player names and every euro figure, with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. Reason: scoreboard and kit-number voice, and long names fit narrow columns.
  - Normal width for UI text (labels, controls, table body, navigation).
- **Source Serif 4** for stored dossier scouting reports (`ReportProse`), `16px` (`15px` in preview), line-height `1.55`. **Source Sans 3** for PitIA's chat answers (`ChatAnswer`), `14px`, line-height `1.55`, `96ch` max measure, matching the UI voice and wider width of the conversation bubbles.
- **Numeric discipline**: tabular numerals on every euro value, percentage, age and match statistic.
- **Scale** (only these six sizes): `11 / 12 / 13 / 15 / 20 / 28` px.
  - `28`: reserved for the two dossier valuation figures (market price and estimate).
  - `20`: page title, dossier player name, archetype title in view 3.
  - `15`: section titles, table player name and price, stat figures, chat answers, brand name.
  - `13`: body and table text, controls, navigation.
  - `12`: secondary lines, status words, labels, buttons, footers.
  - `11`: stat labels and axis labels only.
- **Case**: Spanish sentence case everywhere ("Precio de mercado", "Ahorro estimado"), except proper nouns and module names (`Explorador de Mercado`, `Comparador Moneyball`, `Arquetipos Tácticos`). No wide-tracked uppercase labels.
  - Archetype short labels ("Titular Europeo", "Rotación y Copas", "Titular de Liga") and long card headings ("Titulares de Élite y Exposición Europea", "Perfiles de Rotación y Copas Nacionales", "Titulares Regulares de Liga Doméstica") reflect the empirical K-Means (`k = 3`, `RobustScaler`) competitive-exposure grouping and keep their capitalisation as proper labels from `delphos_domain`.
- **Vocabulary**:
  - The model's estimate (`fair_value_eur`) is "Valor estimado". Never "Valor justo".
  - Statuses (`valuation_status`) are "Infravalorado", "En precio", "Sobrevalorado", identical in the table, filters and comparator.
- **Number format**: Spanish (`es-ES`) through one helper: thousands "5.522", money "€150,0 M" (thousands "€153 mil"), decimals with comma ("1,18"), height "1,84 m", dates "27 may 2026".
- **No impossible figures**: the valuation gap (estimate against price) is a percentage and follows the same rules, in the table's Brecha and the dossier's "Diferencia" alike. A percentage that would round to ±100% without being exactly ±1 gets one decimal ("−99,5%"); if it still rounds to 100 there, it prints "±99,9%" below 100 and "±100,1%" above it. Only exactly ±1 prints ±100%. A share (win, clean sheet, starter, classifier probability) outside 0..1 prints "N/D", and starts above matches played print as "15 (N/D)". Out-of-range values come from upstream data and are not repeated on screen.

## 4. Layout
- **Desktop only**: minimum width `1440px`, designed at `1440x900`. Below 1440 the shell keeps its width and the page scrolls horizontally. The Flutter app owns responsive layouts; this preview does not.
- **Shell**: full-height grid, independent internal scroll containers, no page-level vertical scroll.
- **Sidebar (`240px`, navy; `64px` rail when folded)**:
  - Brand header (`60px`): the hamburger button at the top left, then `DelphosFootball · Dirección Deportiva` in two lines. There is no brand logo or mark in the sidebar; the button takes its place so it is easy to see. The button is `36px`, with `14px` to its left, so it is centred on the `64px` rail and sits in exactly the same place open and folded. It is drawn for the navy ground (three painted lines, no icon font): `--sidebar-text`, white on hover, `--sidebar-active` hover ground and the white focus ring (§2). It is the first stop of the sidebar for the keyboard and keeps focus when it flips the sidebar. Its Spanish label is "Plegar menú" while the sidebar is open and "Desplegar menú" while it is folded (with the matching expanded state), and its tooltip also opens on keyboard focus.
  - The modules as text items, without icons while the sidebar is open: `Explorador de Mercado`, `Comparador Moneyball`, `Arquetipos Tácticos`, `PitIA`. The HTML preview shows only the three data modules, because the PitIA chat is outside the preview (§7).
  - Nav item font weight is constant across states (active is shown with ground and text colour only), so labels never reflow or wrap.
  - The hamburger button in the brand header folds and unfolds the sidebar. The change animates (§5).
  - Folded, the sidebar is a `64px` rail: the hamburger button alone, then one `20px` line icon per module in the same stroke style as the other glyphs (a ledger, two opposed arrows, three groups, a speech bubble; drawn in code, no icon font), in `--sidebar-text` and white on hover. The active module keeps its `--sidebar-active` ground. Each icon has a tooltip with the module's name, which also opens on keyboard focus, and the same Spanish semantics label. The footer is hidden.
  - The folded state is kept in memory for the page's lifetime: it survives moving between modules, not a reload.
  - Footer on one line: `5.522 jugadores · 2025/26`, derived from the data.
- **Top bar**: page title and one line of plain metadata, `Temporada 2025/26 · datos al 5 jun 2026` (latest `as_of_date` in the data). No badges and no controls: the menu button lives in the sidebar's brand header.
- **Filter bar (view 1)**: one row that fits inside the 24px gutters at 1440. Search, then `Estado` as the primary segmented control (`Todos | Infravalorado | En precio | Sobrevalorado`), then `Posición` and `Arquetipo` as selects, then `Restablecer filtros` (enabled only when a filter is active). Option labels match the status words. The search has a placeholder ("Buscar jugador, club o país") and an accessible label, with no visible label, as an explicit exception to the visible-label rule; it is `196px` wide.
- **Master-detail split**: on load only the table shows, at full width. Activating a player's name (or clicking the row) opens the dossier at a fixed `440px`, and the ledger takes the rest (`692px` at 1440). Activating the open player again or the close button closes it, and focus returns to that player's name (or to the table if the row is gone). The selected-row highlight shows only while the dossier is open. If the filters drop the open player, the dossier closes; the selection is kept for the comparator. Opening a player from elsewhere (archetype list, the comparator's back button) opens the dossier and clears any filter that would hide the row. "Ver en el explorador" leaves it closed, scrolls the list to the top and focuses its first player.
  - Escape closes the dossier from anywhere while view 1 is the active view, sidebar and top bar included. It is ignored on a select, which keeps Escape for its list, and on the search field while it has text, which Escape clears first. Focus moves to the player's name only if it was inside the dossier or on the page body; anywhere else it stays where it was.
  - The selected player is shared with the comparator. Picking another player in the comparator's selector changes it, so if the dossier was open, view 1 shows that player's dossier on return. If the filters hide that player, the dossier closes instead, without an announcement while view 1 is hidden.
  - Note for the Flutter app: the real table has 5.522 rows, so a keyboard user must not have to tab through the list to reach an opened dossier. After opening, the app offers a skip action ("Ir a la ficha") or moves focus into the dossier. The preview keeps focus on the name button.
- **Market table (ledger)**: columns `Jugador | Edad | Posición | Mercado | Valor estimado | Brecha`.
  - Row padding `10px 14px`: 10px vertical and 14px at the row edges; 16px between columns (8px per cell side) so six columns fit at 1440.
  - Fixed widths `48 / 142 / 76 / 100 / 112px` for Edad, Posición, Mercado, Valor estimado and Brecha with the dossier open. Brecha has no pill padding to make room for: it fits the widest status word ("Sobrevalorado", `82px` in `12px` Archivo semi-bold) plus the cell's 8px and 14px edges, with 8px to spare. With it closed, Posición widens to `180px` so the longest sub-position ("Extremo izquierdo") fits on one line next to the glyph. Jugador takes the rest. No header wraps at 1440.
  - Jugador: `44x44px` portrait with a `20x20px` crest; then three lines: name, flag + club, archetype as plain muted text (`Titular Europeo`, `Rotación y Copas`, `Titular de Liga`, as published by the clustering stage). Club and archetype do not share a line: together they need up to 226px and the column has about 140px with the dossier open, so a shared line would wrap unevenly.
  - The player name is a real `<button>` styled as text, with `aria-expanded`. The row stays a table row, so screen readers read its cells, and a click anywhere on it is a mouse shortcut. A visually hidden status line announces "Ficha de {nombre} abierta" or "Ficha cerrada" instead of re-reading the panel.
  - Flags: a country with no flag mapping shows no flag, never a stand-in such as the EU flag. Country names in alt text and tooltips are in Spanish.
  - Edad in whole years. Posición: pitch glyph + sub-position (the broad line is in a tooltip; the glyph already shows it).
  - Mercado and Valor estimado right-aligned with tabular numerals; Valor estimado coloured by status.
  - Brecha: the status word (plain coloured text with no ground, §2) over the signed difference of the estimate against the price, `(estimate / price − 1)`, as a percentage ("−65%", "+27%") in secondary text, both right-aligned. It is the same number, from the same formatter and rounding rules (§3 "No impossible figures"), as the dossier's "Diferencia (−65%)", so the table and the dossier always agree. The percentage has no colour of its own and no extra sign mark: the status word carries the verdict, and for En precio a coloured number would show a second one. A player with no price to compare with shows no percentage.
  - Footer on one line: `18 de 5.522 jugadores`.
  - When the explorer opens with a dossier opened elsewhere (archetype list, comparator, review state), that player's row is scrolled into view.
- **Dossier (fixed header + scrollable body)**:
  - **Header** (`flex-shrink: 0`, never clipped): the portrait (`64px`, no crest disc, nothing under it) beside three lines (about `322px` at the `440px` panel). Line 1: the full name with room to breathe (and room for the close button). Line 2: flag and country, " · ", the club crest and club name, " · ", the league logo and league name, with the logos inline before their names. Line 3: the definition row `Edad | Altura | Pie | Posición` with hairline separators, `10px` each side (an item the data lacks is left out). There is no "Activo élite" mark (§2). The logos are `28px` tall at their own aspect ratio, with no disc, frame or shadow; they are decorative, so the text carries the meaning, and the `13px` text keeps the type scale. Line 2 never wraps: if it does not fit, the club name is cut with an ellipsis first, then the country (the full country and club stay in the semantics label); the flag, the league name, the logos and the dots never shrink. Line 3 wraps between items, never inside one, and the item that starts a row has no hairline. A crest or league logo that is missing or fails to load shows nothing and takes no width (while it loads, the line already has its 28px height, so nothing jumps): no broken image, no stand-in. With no season row the league item and its separator are omitted. The league logo is built from `league_id` (`GB1` gives `https://tmssl.akamaized.net/images/logo/header/gb1.png`), only for the leagues in the label table, and is loaded through the same bytes-then-decode path as the crests. With no season row (§7) the age comes from the birth date. One action, in its own slot at the top right: a `32x32px` secondary icon button with a 14px × ("Cerrar ficha"). Only the name line reserves room for it, so the lines below keep the full width of the right column. The header content starts `26px` below the panel's top edge (the button sits `12px` from the top and right), so line 2 starts at least `8px` below the button's bottom edge and lines 2 and 3 never come near it.
  - **Valuation**: "Precio de mercado" and "Valor estimado" at 28px; below them, one line with the status word (plain coloured text, driven only by `valuation_status`), " · " and the gap in plain text ("Diferencia −€130,3 M (−65%)"); there is no dumbbell or other chart; then the classifier check, labelled "Comprobación de los clasificadores": "Random Forest: NN% de probabilidad de infravaloración" from `rf_undervalued_prob`, then one line. If either prediction is missing, the line reads "Sin comprobación de los clasificadores." and says nothing about the status. If Random Forest and the decision tree split (`rf_undervalued_pred` vs `dt_undervalued_pred`): "Random Forest y árbol de decisión no coinciden." If they agree, they are compared with the status (Infravalorado means both predict undervalued; En precio and Sobrevalorado mean both predict not undervalued): "Los dos clasificadores confirman el estado." or "No confirman el estado: los dos clasificadores lo marcan como infravalorado" (or "como no infravalorado"). The status stays the verdict; the check may disagree with it and says so in neutral text.
  - **Season** (`4x2` grid): `Minutos`, `Partidos (titular)`, `Goles`, `Asistencias`, `Victorias`, `Portería imbatida`, `Goles recibidos/p`, `Tarjetas (A/R)`. Subtitle shows the archetype and the player's own `as_of_date`.
  - **Replacements**: up to 3 rows separated by hairlines (portrait, crest, name, similarity, price, savings in ink). Subtitle "Mismo arquetipo y posición · ahorro ≥ 20%". The section header carries the secondary action "Comparar reemplazos", on its own line with `12px` above it (from the subtitle) and `16px` below it (before the first row or the shortfall text). The candidate's club truncates with an ellipsis (full name in a tooltip) so "· NN% de similitud" stays on one line. With fewer than 3 candidates, a plain text line above the rows (no box) states "N de 3 reemplazos" in ink and the reason from `shortfall_reason` in `--ink-2` (§7).
- **Resumen** (the dossier's last section, with no subtitle): the player's scouting report.
  - On open it shows the player's newest stored report and never starts a generation. While the report loads, the section shows its skeleton.
  - No stored report: "Este jugador todavía no tiene informe de scouting." with the action "Generar informe".
  - Generating: one static line, "Generando el informe… N s" (elapsed seconds, tabular figures), with "Cancelar". Screen readers announce "Generando el informe" once, not on every second. The client waits up to 300 s.
  - Failure: "No se pudo generar el informe." after a generation, or "No se pudo cargar el informe." on load, with "Reintentar".
  - Ready: the report prose in Source Serif 4 at `15px` in `--ink-2` (§3), which fades in once. Below it, the provenance line in `12px` `--ink-3`, then "Regenerar".
  - The provenance line is one of three: "Redactado por PitIA (Modelo: Qwen 3.5) - Delphos Football" for a model report, "Redactado por PitIA - Delphos Football" when the model has no display name, and "Plantilla determinista" for the template renderer. It shows no date and no prompt version.
  - The markup is read by an allowlist of paragraphs, bold, italics and line breaks. The report has no images, links or styles (ARCHITECTURE.md ADR-028).
- **Comparador Moneyball (one comparison table)**: metrics as rows; the reference player and up to 3 candidates as columns.
  - The reference column comes first and is set apart by its header (`--surface-muted` header, `--surface-subtle` cells). No colour stripes.
  - Groups: "Valoración" (precio de mercado, valor estimado, estado, ahorro frente a la referencia, similitud táctica) and "Temporada 2025/26" (liga, edad, minutos, goles, asistencias, titular, victorias).
  - Column headers: portrait (with its crest disc), name with flag, and the club. The league is not in the header: the "Liga" row of the Temporada group states it, and repeating it crowded the header. In that row each cell (reference and candidates) shows the league's logo (`16px` tall, no frame) to the left of its name with a `5px` gap; the logo shows nothing and takes no space if it is missing or fails to load, and the name never breaks inside itself ("Premier League"). A player with no season row keeps "Sin datos de temporada", with no logo.
  - The "Estado" row prints each player's status word as plain coloured text (§2), no ground.
  - Candidate cells show a signed delta against the reference, computed from the values as displayed so the arithmetic on screen adds up; share deltas in percentage points ("+8 pp"). The price row has no delta. A value that prints "N/D" (a missing count, or a share out of range, §3) has no delta on either side. Toning per §2.

  - With fewer than 3 candidates, one placeholder column carries "N de 3 reemplazos" and the shortfall text. Never a padded candidate.

  - "Conversaciones" in the header opens the saved conversations, newest first, under "Nueva conversación". Choosing one restores it. Restored answers have no steps. When a conversation is longer than the 200 messages restored, "Mostrando los últimos N mensajes" appears above it.

## 5. Depth and motion
- **Depth**: border only on flat containers (`1px solid var(--rule)`). The one shadow is `--shadow-float`, `0 1px 2px` in ink at 12%, and only layers that float over the page may carry it: tooltips, the comparator's suggestion list, the chat's capabilities dialog, select dropdown menus and the crest disc over a portrait. No card-in-card nesting: sections inside a panel are separated by hairlines and spacing, not by nested bordered boxes.
- **Radius scale** (`DelphosRadius`): `10px` cards, panels and tables; `9px` the clip inside a bordered card; `8px` sidebar items, the menu button and the archetype pitch ground; `7px` controls (inputs, selects, the close button, buttons); `6px` tooltips; `5px` a segment inside the Estado control; `4px` skeleton blocks; `3px` the share bar and the pitch glyph ground; `2px` text links, swatches and flags (a flag also has a `1px` frame in ink at 12%). Circles (avatars, the crest disc) are not on the scale. Every rounded corner uses one of these values.
- **Motion**: one curve, `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, at `160-240ms` (the valuation figures take up to `400ms`). Every motion has one purpose, stated here; no pulsing, and one loop, the chat's shimmer below. These are the only things that move:
  - **Feedback** (`160ms`): hover and press, to confirm the pointer and the click.
  - **Player change** (`180ms`): when one loaded player replaces another inside an open dossier, the content enters at 0.65 opacity and 3px up, to mark that the panel now shows someone else. Each dossier section also fades in once (`160ms`, opacity only) when its data replaces its skeleton.
  - **Dossier panel** (`220ms`): the side panel slides 16px in from the right while it fades in when it opens, and leaves the same way when it closes, with its content on screen throughout, so it is clear where it comes from and where it goes. The table gives up its width at once on open and takes it back when the exit ends. The full-page presentation (narrow windows) only fades in. Focus behaviour on open and close (§4) is unchanged: focus never waits for the animation.
  - **Sidebar** (`240ms`): folding runs one timeline: the labels fade out at the full `240px` (so no text squashes or wraps), then the width eases to `64px` while the icons fade in; unfolding is the same timeline backwards. The hamburger button in the brand header triggers it and neither fades nor moves (its geometry is the same at every point of the fold, and it keeps keyboard focus and its ring); the product name fades with the labels, and the footer ("5.522 jugadores · 2025/26") fades out with them and is not shown on the rail.
  - **View change** (`200ms`): a module page fades in with a 4px rise when it replaces another, to mark the change of view. The sidebar and top bar do not move. Filtering, searching and paging inside the explorer are not view changes.
  - **Valuation figures** (`400ms`, each time the dossier loads a player): "Precio de mercado" and "Valor estimado" count up from €0 to their value through the same es-ES formatter on every frame, with tabular numerals and a fixed width (the widest text of the count) so nothing beside them jitters. There is no chart to animate: the dossier has no dumbbell. It plays each time the dossier loads a player's valuation (a new player, or the same player again after the dossier was closed), never on a rebuild; closing mid-count lets the leaving panel finish its count.
  - **First load** (`200ms` each, `30ms` apart): the first rows of the market table (at most 12, so the last starts at `330ms` and ends before `600ms`) and the three archetype cards rise 6px and 8px while they fade in, to show the data arriving. Only on the first load of the view in a page load: never on filtering, searching, paging, scrolling, or when the user returns to the view.
- **Chat shimmer** (`ShimmerText`, `1.5 s` per pass, looping only while its label is working): a narrow band, lighter than the letters' resting colour, sweeps across the text like a thinking indicator. It marks the one label that is working (the first wait, a running step, or the wait after a result) and it ends with the turn. Under reduced motion the text is static and no ticker runs. This is the only loop in the product. The sweep lightens the letters rather than darkening them toward ink. That was chosen deliberately over a sweep toward ink, which would keep the contrast.
  - Contrast exception: at its peak the band is the resting colour mixed 75% toward `--canvas`, which measures about `1.4:1` for `--ink-3` (`#CECFCE`) and `1.45:1` for `--ink-2` (`#CACBCB`) against canvas, and about `1.6:1` against white. The resting colour keeps the §2 contrast, and the band is only on the letters for the moment it passes.
- **Rules that remain**: nothing loops except the chat shimmer; filters and keystrokes do not animate (typing, choosing a filter, paging and scrolling change the table instantly); no transitions on elements that are re-created by re-rendering, they would never run; skeletons are static blocks, no shimmer, no pulse; every duration goes through one reduced-motion switch.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` cuts every animation and transition to zero: the end state shows at once, with no delay and no stagger.

## 6. Anti-slop guardrails
- **NEVER** display HTTP methods or endpoints (`GET /players`, `POST /reports/generate`), database schemas (`PostgreSQL`, `analytics.*`), or snake_case column names (`residual_log10`, `rf_undervalued_prob`, `win_rate`, `clean_sheet_rate`, `goals_conceded`) anywhere in the UI.
- **NEVER** use Unicode flag emojis (always FlagCDN `<img>` tags) or em dashes.
- **NEVER** invent statistics, confidence scores or labels outside the real dataset contract. If a value is not in the data, it is not on screen.

## 7. States
Implemented in the preview so the Flutter app can copy them. Review one with the URL parameter `?state=loading | error | no-season | shortfall | empty`. With no parameter the preview shows the loaded view; unknown values are ignored. There are no demo controls in the UI.
- **Table loading** (`loading`): 8 static skeleton rows and "Cargando jugadores…" in the table footer; the dossier stays closed, so it has no skeleton; filters are disabled; views 2 and 3 show "Cargando datos".
- **API unreachable** (`error`): one panel across the explorer: "No se pudo conectar con el servidor de datos", one plain sentence, and "Reintentar". The dossier is closed. Retry shows the loading state for 700ms, then the loaded view with the dossier closed, and moves focus to the page title. Filters are disabled, and views 2 and 3 show the same panel.
- **Player without season stats** (`no-season`): opens the first player's dossier, and that player is treated as having no season row.
  - The dossier keeps identity, valuation and replacements, and replaces the stats grid with "Sin estadísticas de temporada para este jugador."
  - Age comes from the birth date at the latest data date, and the league is omitted.
  - In the comparator, the reference's season cells read "Sin datos de temporada" and candidate cells show no deltas.
- **Replacements shortfall** (`shortfall`): opens the dossier of the first player with `qualifying_count < 3` (Rayan in the current data). The text comes from `shortfall_reason`, and codes never reach the UI raw:
  - `no_same_cluster_and_position_peer`: "Ningún otro jugador comparte arquetipo y posición."
  - `no_peer_within_athletic_distance`: "Ningún jugador del mismo arquetipo y posición está dentro de la distancia de similitud exigida."
  - `no_peer_below_cost_ceiling`: "Ningún jugador similar cuesta al menos un 20% menos."
  - `no_non_overvalued_peer`: "Los jugadores similares y más baratos están sobrevalorados."
  - `fewer_than_three_qualifying_peers`: "Solo N jugadores cumplen todos los criterios: mismo arquetipo y posición, similitud suficiente, ahorro mínimo del 20% y no sobrevalorado."
  - Any other code: a generic "Solo N jugadores cumplen los criterios de reemplazo."
- **Filtered to empty** (`empty`): the filters are preset to the first status and archetype pair with no rows. The message names the active filters ("Filtros activos: estado En precio · arquetipo Titular de Liga."), next to "Restablecer filtros". The dossier is closed: it never shows a player the table does not list.
- **Out of scope**: the preview shows neither the Resumen nor the PitIA chat. Their states are specified in §4, and the Flutter app is their reference. The endpoints are `POST /reports/generate` and `POST /chat` (delivery priority 4 in ADR-007).
